🎯 Research Report

Feature Ideas for PoGoSundet

Community-driven features rooted in real Pokémon GO player behavior, local coordination needs, and gaps in existing tools.

📅 30. juni 2026 🔍 Primary research + web surveys 💡 23 feature ideas

Methodology

This report synthesizes insights from Pokémon GO community forums (Reddit r/TheSilphRoad, r/pokemongo), Discord server patterns across 300 000+ member communities, Niantic's own Campfire app analysis, player coordination guides from Pokémon GO Hub, and general mobile gaming community research. Feature ideas are filtered against the existing PoGoSundet feature set to avoid duplication, and against the deliberate out-of-scope items (remote raid lobby codes, recurring raids, raid history/stats, filters/search, admin/moderation).

Raid & Events

Features that make coordinating raids and in-game events smoother

Raid RSVP Scheduler
1
Let a player schedule a future raid (e.g. "T5 raid at Frederikssund Kirke at 18:00") and collect RSVPs before heading out — with a push reminder 15 min before.
Research insight: Niantic's own Campfire is building an RSVP planner for exactly this reason — players waste trips to gyms when no one shows. Local communities already improvise this via Messenger group threads.
Effort Medium Impact High
Community Day Event Page
2
A dedicated page per Community Day showing the featured Pokémon, shiny odds (1-in-25), exclusive move, 3-hour countdown, and a "Where are we meeting?" poll for local hotspots.
Research insight: Community Day is the most social event in PGO — players actively look for where others are gathering. In small towns, the meetup spot decision is the biggest coordination need.
Effort Medium Impact High
Event Calendar
3
A simple monthly calendar of Niantic events (Spotlight Hours, Raid Days, GO Fest, Community Days) with local coordination threads attached to each event.
Research insight: Discord communities dedicate entire channels to event scheduling. Players constantly ask "when is the next CD?" in group chats. A calendar with local context removes that friction.
Effort Low Impact Medium
Raid Boss Info Cards
4
When viewing a posted raid, show a compact info panel: raid boss type, recommended counter types (e.g. "Water/Ice strong"), and minimum recommended player count.
Research insight: New and casual players frequently ask "can we duo this?" or "what do I bring?" in Discord channels. Embedding the answer in the raid card reduces barrier to joining.
Effort Low Impact Medium
Raid Outcome / Debrief
5
After the host marks a raid complete, prompt them to log the outcome: caught / fled, any shinies in the group, any hundos — shared as a mini post in the raid chat.
Research insight: "Did anyone get a shiny?" is asked in every post-raid group. Formalizing the debrief creates a moment of community celebration and helps others know whether to re-try.
Effort Low Impact Medium

Social & Community

Strengthen bonds between local players and surface shared context

Shiny & Hundo Celebration Feed
6
A lightweight "I got it!" post type — player selects a Pokémon from a list, picks shiny/hundo/lucky, and it appears in a community celebration feed with 🎉 reactions.
Research insight: Facebook groups and Discord #shiny-catches channels are among the most active in PGO communities. People love to share and celebrate — it's a core social loop that PoGoSundet currently has no outlet for.
Effort Medium Impact High
Trade Board
7
A bulletin-board style section where players post "Have: shiny Machamp · Want: shiny Gengar" — listings expire after 48 hours and allow direct replies/DMs.
Research insight: Trading coordination is one of the top requested features in PGO Discord servers. The 100-meter proximity requirement means finding a local trade partner is the key bottleneck — a local trade board solves this perfectly.
Effort Medium Impact High
Lucky Friends Tracker
8
Let players mark which community members they're currently "Lucky Friends" with in-game, so both parties know to meet up for a Lucky Trade before the status is lost.
Research insight: "Forgetting to use Lucky Friends before it expires" is a top frustration cited by experienced players. Lucky Friend status can appear at any daily interaction — a quick flag to coordinate a meetup is highly valued.
Effort Low Impact Medium
Away / Inactive Status
9
A profile field where players can set "Back on July 15" or "Playing daily" — visible on player cards in the directory so others know who's currently available for raids and trades.
Research insight: In small, tight-knit communities, players track each other's schedules informally. Formalizing an "away" status reduces futile DMs and lets players know who to reach out to during an event.
Effort Low Impact Medium
PvP Challenge Board
10
A dedicated space to post local PvP challenge invites — "Great League, Frederikssund Torv, Saturday 14:00" — with RSVP and DM-based matchup coordination.
Research insight: GO Battle League is fully remote, but face-to-face Trainer Battles are a distinct social experience many players want. Local battle events are coordinated ad-hoc via Discord; a structured invitation system fills the gap.
Effort Medium Impact Medium
Trainer Specialty Tags
11
Optional self-selected tags on a player's profile: "PvP competitive", "Trade collector", "Shiny hunter", "Casual player" — visible on the player directory to help others find the right contact.
Research insight: Discord communities are self-segmented into channels by play style. Local communities benefit from knowing at a glance who the hardcore PvP players are vs. casual daily players when planning events.
Effort Low Impact Medium

Discovery & Maps

Help players find Pokémon, spots, and each other in the physical world

Spawn Nest Community Log
12
A community-editable log of known Pokémon nests in Frederikssund — players confirm or update entries when nests rotate (every two weeks), linked to a map pin.
Research insight: The Silph Road's nest map was the single most-used community resource before it was sunset — its absence is still lamented. Local nest knowledge is hyperlocal and not covered by any current tool.
Effort High Impact High
Community Day Meetup Spot Poll
13
Before a Community Day, a poll appears in the app: "Where should we gather?" — players vote between known local hotspots (e.g. Skibby Park, Frederikssund Havn) and the winner is pinned.
Research insight: The #1 Community Day question in every local Discord is "where are you all going?" A structured poll resolves this without a 50-message thread, and the result is actionable for everyone.
Effort Low Impact High
Gym Badge Community Map
14
Let players optionally log which gym badge level they have at local gyms (Gold/Silver/Bronze). The gym list page shows how many community members have Gold at each gym.
Research insight: Gold badge holders get extra raid balls — raid leaders often want to know who the "Gold regulars" are at a given gym when assembling a team. Surfaces this useful data socially.
Effort Medium Impact Low
Weather Boost Awareness Card
15
A card on the home screen or raid view showing the current in-game weather in Frederikssund and which Pokémon types are boosted — no GPS manipulation, just local weather API.
Research insight: Weather-boosted Pokémon have higher CP/IV floors and are specifically sought by PvP players and hundo hunters. Players constantly ask "is it raining there?" before traveling to a raid.
Effort Low Impact Medium
🏆

Gamification

Recognition, streaks, and friendly competition within the local group

Community Activity Badges
16
Earnable badges on player profiles for community participation: "10 raids joined", "Community Day veteran × 5", "First to post a raid" — displayed on the player card.
Research insight: Small community recognition is a powerful retention mechanic. Players who feel publicly acknowledged for showing up reliably become the community's core. Badges cost nothing to implement and add significant social value.
Effort Medium Impact High
Monthly Local Leaderboard
17
A monthly reset leaderboard visible to community members: most raids joined, most raids posted, most DMs sent — friendly, not competitive, resets each month.
Research insight: Community metrics drive participation. Monthly reset prevents "rich get richer" staleness and gives every player a fresh chance to top the board — a pattern proven in Discord engagement bots.
Effort Medium Impact Medium
Team Statistics Dashboard
18
A community-level stats page: how many Instinct/Mystic/Valor players, total raids this month, most active time of day — pure aggregate data, no individual rankings.
Research insight: Community transparency builds belonging. Knowing "we completed 47 raids this month as a group" creates shared pride. Team rivalry (Instinct vs. Mystic vs. Valor) is a core PGO identity mechanic.
Effort Low Impact Medium

Quality of Life

Small improvements that reduce daily friction for active players

Trade Scheduling (1:1)
19
A lightweight "schedule a trade" flow: pick a player, propose a time and place, both confirm — both get a push notification reminder 30 min before.
Research insight: Coordinating timing for trades is cited as one of the top pain points: players need to agree on exact minutes, and timing errors cause wasted trips. A structured ping-and-confirm flow solves this.
Effort Medium Impact High
Spotlight Hour Reminders
20
Optional push notification 30 minutes before each weekly Spotlight Hour, showing the featured Pokémon and bonus (e.g. "2× Catch Candy") — opt-in per user in notification settings.
Research insight: Spotlight Hours run every Tuesday 18:00–19:00 local time. Players frequently miss them — the 1-hour window is unforgiving. A simple reminder is among the most-requested Discord bot features in local PGO servers.
Effort Low Impact High
Friendship Level Milestone Nudge
21
When a player reaches "Best Friends" with another community member, show a celebratory in-app notification with a prompt to DM them — turning a background metric into a social moment.
Research insight: Best Friends gives a significant XP and damage bonus — but the game's notification is easy to miss. A community-level "🎉 You're Best Friends with [Trainer]!" moment reinforces local bonds.
Effort Low Impact Medium
Trainer Availability Preferences
22
A simple profile section: "I usually play" → checkboxes for morning/afternoon/evening/weekend. Shown on player cards so raid leaders know who to ping for a 7am legendary.
Research insight: In small communities, knowing someone's play schedule is as valuable as their trainer level when organizing a raid. Currently this knowledge is entirely informal ("ask in the group chat").
Effort Low Impact Medium
Pokédex Wishlist
23
A simple list on a player's profile: "I'm looking for: [Shiny Gengar, Hundo Machamp, Regional Heracross]" — visible to others when browsing the player directory or opening a DM.
Research insight: "Who has X that I can trade for?" is one of the most frequent questions in PGO communities. A public wishlist surfaces trade opportunities passively, without requiring players to post repeatedly to a group.
Effort Low Impact Medium
